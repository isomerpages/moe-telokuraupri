<p>As Telok Kurau Primary School celebrates its 100<sup>th</sup> anniversary,
we honour our meaningful connection with Maitri School. Maitri School is
a Special Education school established by Metta Welfare Association (MWA),
a registered charity and the school’s parent organisation in November 2019.&nbsp;
As such, the <a href="http://Giving.sg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Giving.sg</a> campaign
is hosted under MWA, with all donations directed towards supporting Maitri
School and its students.</p>
<p>Maitri School is currently sited at the former Eunos Primary School campus.
This campus serves as Maitri School’s holding site until it moves to its
new premise in the future.</p>
<p>Maitri School supports students aged 7 to 18 with moderate to severe Autism
Spectrum Disorder (ASD), providing specialised education that develops
communication, independence, social skills and lifelong learning.</p>
<p>In celebration of our centennial, we invite TKPS community to contribute
towards enriching the learning experiences of Maitri School students. Every
contribution, regardless of the amount, can help create meaningful opportunities
for them to learn, grow and reach their fullest potential.</p>
<p>Those who wish to contribute may donate through the link <a href="https://www.giving.sg/donate/campaign/fundraising-to-enrich-the-learning-experiences-of-maitri-school-students." rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">https://www.giving.sg/donate/campaign/fundraising-to-enrich-the-learning-experiences-of-maitri-school-students.</a>
</p>
<p>Your support will help Maitri students:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Access communication and language learning resources,</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Develop confidence and positive relationships through social-emotional
learning,</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Explore creativity and self-expression through the performing arts, visual
arts and enrichment experiences and</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Build independence through daily living skills such as meal preparation
and self-care.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Donations are open from now until 31 October 2026. Together, let us make
a meaningful difference in the lives and learning experiences of Maitri
students.</p>
